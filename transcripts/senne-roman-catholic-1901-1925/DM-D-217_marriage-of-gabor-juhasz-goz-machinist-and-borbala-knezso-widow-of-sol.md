## Translation

No. 3. On 7 February 1907: Gábor Juhász Göz, machinist, born at Tiszaszőllős; of Solymos 72, Reformed, born 8 January 1882, bachelor; son of Borbála Juhász G., a single woman, of Tiszaszőllős; married Borbála Knezso, widow of Mihály Jaczko, of Solymos 35, Roman Catholic, born 18 September 1873; daughter of Mihály Knezso, Reformed, farmer, and Mária Benyik, Roman Catholic, of Solymos. Witnesses Gergely Knezso, Greek Catholic, and János Knezso, Roman Catholic, farmers of Solymos. A file number, 16.

## Notes

- The bride was born on 18 September 1873. That is the Borbála baptised at Solymos 19 with the mother written 'Olaxsik Mária' (DM-D-172). Here the mother is Benyik Mária. One child, two surnames for her mother: Mária Olaxsik and Mária Benyik are the same woman.
- A sister of Mária Knyezső of 1880. One witness is her brother János; the other is Gergely Knezso, Greek Catholic, the 'Hreha' of the census. So the Reformed, Roman Catholic and Greek Catholic Knezsos of Solymos stood at one another's weddings.
