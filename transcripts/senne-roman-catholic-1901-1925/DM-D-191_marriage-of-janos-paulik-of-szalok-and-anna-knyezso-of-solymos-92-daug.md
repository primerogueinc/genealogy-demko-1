## Translation

No. 7. On 31 May 1904: János Paulik, farmer, of Szalók in Zemplén county, no. 35, Roman Catholic, born 18 October 1880, bachelor; son of János Paulik, farmer of Szalók, and Anna Matyi; married Anna Knyezső, of Solymos 92, Roman Catholic, born 20 June 1887, maiden; daughter of Pál Knyezső, Reformed, farmer of Solymos, and Mária Cservenyák, Roman Catholic. Witnesses Antal Hrenyo and János Mitrik. Banns read three times; the groom released by his own parish.
