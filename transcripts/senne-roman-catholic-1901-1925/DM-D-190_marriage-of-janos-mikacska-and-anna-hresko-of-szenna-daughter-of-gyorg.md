## Translation

No. 15. On 7 November 1904: János Mikácska (?), cottar, of Csecsehó 15, Greek Catholic, born 20 June 1879 (?), bachelor; son of János Mikácska (?), servant of Csecsehó, and Anna Pavo (?); married Anna Hresko, of Szenna 72, Roman Catholic, born 29 August 1883, maiden; daughter of György Hresko, farmer of Szenna, and Anna Matyi, Greek Catholic. Witnesses János Pado and Mihály Pavo (?), of Csecsehó. Banns read three times; the groom released by his own parish.
