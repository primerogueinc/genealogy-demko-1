## Translation

No. 8. On 23 May 1921: Mihály Danko, of Ungszenna 140 (?), Greek Catholic, born 1 November 1896, bachelor; son of János Danko and Mária Nyemcsik; married Zsuzsanna Kocsis, of Ungszenna 111, Roman Catholic, born 24 August 1899, maiden; daughter of Mihály Kocsis and Anna Dvonyik. Witnesses György Sztriczko and György Raskovki. Banns read three times; a certificate of banns from the groom's own priest.
