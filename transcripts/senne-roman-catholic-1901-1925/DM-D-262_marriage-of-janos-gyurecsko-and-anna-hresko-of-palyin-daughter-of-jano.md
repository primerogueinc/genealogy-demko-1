## Translation

No. 5. On 12 February 1918: János Gyurecsko, of Pályin 88, Roman Catholic, born 28 April 1895, bachelor; son of János Gyurecsko and Zsuzsa Magyar Ondo; married Anna Hresko, of Pályin, Reformed, born 30 June 1897, maiden; daughter of János Hresko, Roman Catholic, and Zsuzsanna Vircsik, Reformed. Witnesses György Gyurcsik and Mihály Hornyák. With a dispensation from the diocese for the difference of religion.
