## Translation

No. 2. On 10 February 1902: Mihály Hresko, farmer, of Pályin 40, Roman Catholic, born 27 May 1876, bachelor; son of János Hresko, farmer of Pályin, and Anna Takács; married Borbála Gyurecsko, of Pályin 32, Roman Catholic, born 11 February 1878, maiden; daughter of András Gyurecsko, farmer of Pályin, and Borbála Poti (?), Reformed. Witnesses János Vircsik and Pál Valyócsik, of Pályin. Banns read three times.
