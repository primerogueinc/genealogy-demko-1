## Translation

No. 11. On 29 August 1904: Mihály Hresko Minarcsin, servant, of Szenna 1, Roman Catholic, born 22 August 1881, bachelor; son of János Hresko Minarcsin, servant of Szenna, and Mária Raskóvki; married Julia Horni, born at Pályin, of Szenna 27, born 29 August 1886, maiden; daughter of András Hornyák, smith, and Anna Nyemcsik, of Varannó in Zemplén. Witnesses György Duska, cottar, and Mihály Duska, miller, of Szenna. Banns read three times.
