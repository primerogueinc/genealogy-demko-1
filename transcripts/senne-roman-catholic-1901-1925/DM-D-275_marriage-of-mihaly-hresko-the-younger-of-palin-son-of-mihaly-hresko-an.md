## Translation

No. 9. On 2 June 1925: Mihály Hresko, of Palín 125, Roman Catholic, born 1 April 1904, bachelor; son of Mihály Hresko and Borbála Gyurecsko; married Anna Gyurecsko, of Palín 136, Roman Catholic, born 24 June 1908, maiden; daughter of Mihály Gyurecsko and Ilona Kocela (?). Witnesses János Harbulyák and János Gyurecsko. Banns read three times.
