## Translation

No. 5. On 25 May 1903: János Nyemcsik, cottar, of Nagy Szeretva 47, born 24 March 1881, bachelor; son of János Nyemcsik, cottar of Szenna, and Anna Komanik (?); married Zsuzsanna Kiszlán, of Nagy Szeretva 57, born 9 June 1882, maiden; daughter of György Kiszlán and Mária Polyo (?). Witnesses Mihály Szabovik and János Isk., of Nagy Szeretva. Banns read three times.
