## Translation

No. 18. On 22 September 1925: András Ihnát, of Jesztreb, Greek Catholic, born 18 January 1902, bachelor; son of György Ihnát and Anna Farkas; married Mária Buska (?), of Jesztreb, Roman Catholic, born 3 December 1908, maiden; daughter of János Buska (?) and Mária Kolyibas (?). Witnesses Mihály Kalyibas and János Safin. Performed by commission by a priest of Solymos; banns read three times.
