## Translation

No. 3. On 20 February 1905: György Kocsis, farmer, of Solymos 98, Greek Catholic, born 8 February 1882, bachelor; son of György Kocsis, Greek Catholic farmer of Solymos, and Anna Ivankó; married Borbála Gazso, of Solymos 19 (?), Roman Catholic, born 6 October 1888, maiden; daughter of János Gazso, Greek Catholic farmer of Solymos, and Susanna Mechir. Witnesses János Ádám, farmer of Solymos, and András Polyák, sexton of Senné. Banns read three times; the groom released by his own parish.

## Notes

- The entry before it in the book, no. 2 of the same day, is the marriage of his brother Mihály. DM-H-050 is settled.
- The bride's house looks like 19. If so, house 19 of Solymos held a Gazso family in 1905, a quarter of a century after Mária Knyezső married from it. Doubtful figure.
