## Translation

No. 12. On 26 October 1903: János Knyezső, farmer, of Solymos 27, Roman Catholic, born 24 June 1883, bachelor; son of Mihály Knyezső, Reformed, farmer of Solymos, and Mária Benyik, Roman Catholic; married Anna Ádám, of Solymos 33, Roman Catholic, born 7 September 1887, maiden; daughter of Mihály Ádám, farmer of Solymos, and Susanna Estók, Reformed. Witnesses András Polyák, sexton of Senné, and János Ádám, cottar of Solymos. Banns read three times.
