## Translation

No. 2. On 29 January 1913: Miklós Duska, cottar, of Ungszenna, born 28 November 1890, bachelor; son of György Duska, cottar, and Erzsébet Schvarcz; married Mária Hankuscsák, of Ungszenna, born 18 April 1892 (?), maiden; daughter of András Hankuscsák, farmer, and Mária Nyemcsik. Witnesses Mihály Murnyik and János Muha. Banns read three times.
