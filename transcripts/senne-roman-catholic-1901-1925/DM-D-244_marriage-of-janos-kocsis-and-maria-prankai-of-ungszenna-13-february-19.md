## Translation

No. 4. On 13 February 1911: János Kocsis, cottar, of Ungszenna 23, Roman Catholic, born 27 July 1889 (?), bachelor; son of a cottar whose name is cut off, and Erzsébet Marcinko; married Mária Prankai, a cottar's daughter, of Ungszenna 60, born 9 January 1893, maiden; daughter of György Prankai, cottar, and Paulina Korik (?). Witnesses János Duska and András Raskovki. Banns read three times.
