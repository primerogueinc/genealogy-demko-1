## Translation

No. 7. On 12 July 1917: János Kosicsán (?), farmer, born in America, Pennsylvania, at a place beginning Bradd; of Pálócz, Greek Catholic, born 26 February 1898, bachelor; son of Mihály Kosicsán and Borbála Sisz (?), of Pálócz; married Mária Mazur, of Alsókánya, Roman Catholic, born 29 August 1899, maiden; daughter of György Mazur and Anna Ihnát. Witnesses András Kicska and Mihály Szerencsko. Banns read three times; a certificate of banns from the groom's own priest.
