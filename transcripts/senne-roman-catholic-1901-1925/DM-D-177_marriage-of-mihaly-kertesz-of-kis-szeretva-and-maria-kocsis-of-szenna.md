## Translation

No. 2. On 20 May 1901: Mihály Kertész, farmer, of Kis Szeretva 27 (?), Greek Catholic, born 8 September 1876, bachelor; son of Mihály Kertész, farmer of Kis Szeretva, and Anna Klovanik (?); married Mária Kocsis, of Szenna 19 (?), Roman Catholic, born 28 February 1884, maiden; daughter of György Kocsis, cottar of Szenna, and Anna Marcinko. Witnesses János Géber and János Volosin, of Kis Szeretva. Banns read three times; the groom released by his own parish.
