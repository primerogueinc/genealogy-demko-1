## Translation

No. 2. On 20 February 1905: Mihály Kocsis, farmer, of Solymos 98, Greek Catholic, born 20 September 1879, bachelor; son of György Kocsis, Greek Catholic farmer of Solymos, and Anna Ivankó; married Anna Tejfák (?), of Solymos 87 (?), Roman Catholic, born 15 November 1885, maiden; daughter of János Tejfák (?), Greek Catholic farmer of Solymos, and Susanna Stabi (?), of Szenna. Witnesses János Ádám, farmer of Solymos, and András Polyák, sexton of Senné. Banns read three times; the groom released by his own parish.
