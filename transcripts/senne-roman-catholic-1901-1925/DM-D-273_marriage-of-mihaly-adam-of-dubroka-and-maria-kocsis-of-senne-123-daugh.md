## Translation

No. 2. On 18 February 1924: Mihály Ádám, of Dubróka 134, Greek Catholic, born 15 October 1901, bachelor; son of Mihály Ádám and Mária Petro, of Dubróka; married Mária Kocsis, of Senné 123, Roman Catholic, born 27 February 1906, maiden; daughter of Mihály Kocsis, farmer, and Anna Dvornyik, of Senné. Witnesses János Novák and Péter Ádám. Banns read three times; a certificate of banns from the groom's own priest.
