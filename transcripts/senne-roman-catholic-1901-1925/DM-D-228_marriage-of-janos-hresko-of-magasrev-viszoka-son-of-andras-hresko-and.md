## Translation

No. 16. On 3 November 1908: János Hreskó, a young farmer, of Magasrév, Roman Catholic, bachelor; his birthday is not on the strip; son of András Hresko, Roman Catholic farmer, and Mária Vircsik, of Magasrév; married Anna Hankuscsik, of Ungszenna, Roman Catholic, born 27 October 1890, maiden; daughter of József Hankuscsik and Anna Jakub. Witnesses Mihály Révész and János Szabados, farmers of Magasrév. Banns read three times; a certificate of banns from the groom's priest.
