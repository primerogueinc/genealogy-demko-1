## Translation

No. 14. On 24 October 1904: Mihály Knyezső, farmer, of Solymos 110 (?), Greek Catholic, born 15 July 1882, bachelor; son of Gergely Knyezső, Greek Catholic, of Solymos, and Borbála Halász, Roman Catholic, of Szenna; married Mária Padó, widow of György Cservenyák, Greek Catholic farmer, of Jesztreb 59, Roman Catholic, born 17 June 1885; daughter of János Padó, farmer of Csecsehó, and Mária Mráz. Witnesses János Knyezső, Greek Catholic farmer of Solymos, and András Polyák, sexton. Banns read three times.

## Notes

- This is the couple of Iňačovce no. 145 in the census of 1930 (DM-D-159): Michal Kňežo, Greek Catholic, born July 1882, and his Roman Catholic wife Maria, born 1885 at Jastrabie, married in the autumn of 1904. The census said 30 July and September; the book says 15 July and 24 October.
- So the old father 'Hreha' of that sheet is Gergely Knyezső, husband of Borbála Halász (DM-X-050). She died in January 1919 (see the burial in this batch). The 'mother Anna, married October 1881' of the census is then a second wife who gave a wrong date, or the census row is misread. Not settled.
