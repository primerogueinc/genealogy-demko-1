## Translation

No. 18. On 22 July 1918: János Dvonyik, of Ungszenna 58, born 22 May 1889, bachelor; son of a father whose name is cut off, and Erzsébet Pupik; married Anna Kocsis, of Ungszenna 111, born 26 December 1895, maiden; daughter of Mihály Kocsis and Anna Dvonyik. Witnesses János Iski and Mihály Szacsko. Banns read three times; a certificate of banns from the groom's priest.
