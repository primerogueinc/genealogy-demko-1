## Translation

No. 11. On 14 November 1880 János Kocsis, bachelor, 22, Greek Catholic, of Solymos, son of the farmers Pál Kocsis and Anna Cservenyák, married Mária Knyezső, maiden, 18, Roman Catholic, of Solymos 19, daughter of the farmers Mihály Knyezső and Mária Olaxák. Witnesses Mihály Kocsis and Mihály Kenderes. The banns were read.

## Notes

- Read from the image before any entry for this strip existed in the Duska repo (none at @9794d20). The owner's file name, which states the relationships, was seen first; so was the Duska agent's note listing it. The expectation was written down before the image was opened: field-notes/before-the-senne-strips.md, commit 6665389.
- House 19 is the house where a 'Mihály Kocsis, Greek Catholic' and 'Mária Knyezső, Roman Catholic' baptise daughters in [1887] and [1889] (DM-D-054, DM-D-048, both looked at again in this batch: the father's name there is plainly Mihály). So the bride is that mother, and this entry names her parents.
- The groom is János. The first witness is a Mihály Kocsis. A Mihály was baptised to this same Pál Kocsis and Anna Cservenyák in [1847 or 1848] (DM-D-059); he would be 32, not 22. So the witness is probably the groom's elder brother. Why the Greek Catholic book then calls the husband Mihály is not settled: DM-H-054.
- The widow Kočišová of the census of 1930 gave her wedding day as 10 November 1879 and her birth as 15 August 1863 (DM-D-105). This is 14 November 1880, the bride 18. A year and four days out; taken as the same wedding.
- The owner's file name has the year and both sets of parents right. It reads the groom's mother as 'Ceternoyak' and the bride's as 'Olaxsek'.
