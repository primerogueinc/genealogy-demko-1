No. 14, 3 February [1879]: Zsuzsanna, legitimate daughter of Mihály Knyezső, of the Helvetic confession, and Mária Olaxsik, Roman Catholic, of Solymos 19. Godparents Mihály Ádám and Anna Palusik.
