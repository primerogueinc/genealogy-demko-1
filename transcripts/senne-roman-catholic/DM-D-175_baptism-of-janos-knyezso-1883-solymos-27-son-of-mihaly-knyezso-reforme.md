## Translation

No. 31, 1883: János, legitimate son of Mihály Knyezső, of the Helvetic confession, and Mária [B]enyák, Roman Catholic, farmers, of Solymos 27. Godparents Mihály Ádám and Zsuzsanna Kenderes. Baptised by the parish priest György Balás.

## Notes

- Read from the image before any entry for this strip existed in the Duska repo (none at @9794d20). The expectation was written down before the image was opened: field-notes/before-the-senne-strips.md, commit 6665389.
