## Translation

Iňačovce no. 14, 1 December 1930. Juraj Kniežo, Calvinist, born 3 April 1856, owner; his wife Anna, Greek Catholic, born 1860, married 15 January 1878, six children of whom five are dead. A daughter-in-law Anna, born 1888, married 1907, and her daughter Anna Onderko, born 1910, married 1927.

## Notes

- A Reformed husband and a Greek Catholic wife, married in 1878: a mixed Kniežo marriage of the very years in which the Roman Catholic Mária Knyezső married a Greek Catholic Kocsis.
- The Duska agent wrote 'six children, five living' and the month of the 1907 wedding as November.
- Columns 9 and 10 are 'all children born alive' and 'of them dead', for the last marriage. Read so here from this batch on.
- Anyone born after November 1926 is given by year only.
- Not an independent reading: the Duska agent routed this sheet to this line and its abstract was open to this agent. Read here from the image first, then compared with DUS:D-011-75.
