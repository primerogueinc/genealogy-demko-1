## Translation

Iňačovce no. 165, 1 December 1930. Ján Kňežo, Calvinist, born 5 December 1900, owner; his wife Anna from Blatné Remety, born 1904, married 1919, three children, one dead; daughters Anna (1924) and Maria (1926). His mother Anna, born 1870 at Hažín, married 1890, widowed 1922, ten children of whom eight are dead. A nephew Ján Kováč, born 1909 at Hažín.
