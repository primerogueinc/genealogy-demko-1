## Translation

Iňačovce no. 144, 1 December 1930. Ján Kňežo, Calvinist, born June 1881, owner; his wife Anna, born 1885, married 1902, three children, one dead. Their daughter Maria, born 1908, her husband Ján Tomovčík from Jastrabie, married 1925, and two small sons. The owner's father Pavol Kňežo, widower, born 12 November 1850.
