## Translation

Iňačovce no. 31, 1 December 1930. Juraj Kniežo, Calvinist, born 5 March 1896, owner; his wife Anna, born 1894, married May 1921; daughters Anna (1922) and Maria (1929). His father Michal Kniežo, widower, born 22 July 1857. Lodging with them, 'no relation': Anna Marcinek, Roman Catholic widow, born at Senné in 1859.
