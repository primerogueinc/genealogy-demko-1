## Translation

Iňačovce no. 167, 1 December 1930. Maria Danková, born 8 December 1891, married 1912, her husband not in the house; her sons Juraj (1914) and Ján (1918), Greek Catholic. Her mother Anna Kňežová, Greek Catholic, a widow since 1910, born 14 June 1848, married 16 February 1872.

## Notes

- The owner's label says 'House of Maria Demkova'. The surname on the sheet is Danková. No Demko is in this house; she is a Kňežo by birth.
- The Duska agent reads Danková too (DUS:D-011-79).
- Columns 9 and 10 are 'all children born alive' and 'of them dead', for the last marriage. Read so here from this batch on.
