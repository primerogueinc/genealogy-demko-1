## Translation

Vysoká, house 8, on the night of 1 December 1930. Barbora, widow Hrobáková, born Demková, owner of the dwelling, born 10 April 1879 at Vysoká, married 20 June 1904, widowed 25 March 1915, three children born. Her sons Michal Hrobák, born 15 February 1912, and Ján Hrobák, born 22 November 1914, both single.

## Notes

- The commissioner wrote her maiden name: 'r. Demková', born Demko. A Borbála Demkó was baptised at Viszoka on 23 April [1879], at house 121, daughter of György Demkó and Mária Hreskó (DM-D-015). Same name, village, month and year; the day is thirteen days off. She is taken to be that child, a sister of Maria Demko's father (DM-H-045).
- The owner's file name calls this the 'House of Borbara Demkova'. On the sheet her surname is Hrobáková and Demková is the name she was born with.
- She was widowed on 25 March 1915, with a son of three and another of four months. The sheet gives no cause. From recall, not checked: that is the eighth month of the war, and men of her husband's age were at the front.
- The right-hand columns are faint in this capture; rite and occupation are read with doubt.
- Not an independent reading: the Duska agent's note named this house in an earlier batch. Read from the image; agrees with DUS:D-011-104 in every field read by both.
