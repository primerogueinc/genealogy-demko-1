## Translation

Iňačovce no. 3, 1 December 1930. Pavel Kniežo the younger, Calvinist, born 20 February 1871, owner; his wife Zuzana, born Tomovčík, born 1877, married 1895. Their son Ján, born 1897; his wife Mária, born Eštok, born 1900, married 1920, four children of whom one is dead; sons Ján (1926) and the twins Štefan and Josef (1928).

## Notes

- This commissioner wrote a second name small under the surname: the maiden name under each wife, and under the son the same name as under his mother. So the small name marks whose branch a Kniežo is.
- The Reformed baptism book of Solymos has the same habit: 'Knyezső Estók' in 1904 (DM-X-054).
- Four born and one dead leaves three, and three sons are in the house: the check that shows what the column means.
- Columns 9 and 10 are 'all children born alive' and 'of them dead', for the last marriage. Read so here from this batch on.
- Anyone born after November 1926 is given by year only.
