## Translation

Iňačovce no. 17, 1 December 1930. Ján Kniežo, with 'kontra' written under his surname, Reformed, born 13 March 1886, owner; his wife Anna from Blatné Remety, born 1894, married February 1911, five children of whom one is dead; Štefan (1921), Andrej (1925), Anna (1927) and Eduard (1930).

## Notes

- 'kontra' is in the place where this commissioner writes a mother's or a wife's family name (see no. 3). A György Knyezső and Anna Kontra have children in the Reformed book of Solymos in 1892 and 1894 (DM-X-054); a son of theirs born in 1886 would be this man. Not shown.
- It bears on the DNA lead of DM-H-049 only this far: the Kontra name is tied to a Reformed Knyezső house, and Mária Knyezső was Roman Catholic.
- The Duska agent reads 'also called Kontra' and the wedding day as 21.
- Columns 9 and 10 are 'all children born alive' and 'of them dead', for the last marriage. Read so here from this batch on.
- Anyone born after November 1926 is given by year only.
