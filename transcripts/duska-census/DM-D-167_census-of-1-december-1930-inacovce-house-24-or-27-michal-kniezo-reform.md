## Translation

Iňačovce no. 24 or 27, 1 December 1930. Michal Kniežo, Calvinist, born 17 September 1899, owner; his wife Anna from Hažín, born 1901, married 17 May 1919; Michal (1923) and Anna (1926). His mother Anna, born 1869, widowed 23 July 1907, five children of whom two are dead.

## Notes

- The house number is read 24 here and 27 by the Duska agent. Not settled.
- Columns 9 and 10 are 'all children born alive' and 'of them dead', for the last marriage. Read so here from this batch on.
- Anyone born after November 1926 is given by year only.
- Not an independent reading: the Duska agent routed this sheet to this line and its abstract was open to this agent. Read here from the image first, then compared with DUS:D-011-88.
