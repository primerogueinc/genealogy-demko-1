## Translation

Iňačovce no. 146, 1 December 1930. Ján Kňežo, Greek Catholic farmer, born 10 February 1881, owner. His wife Anna, Roman Catholic, born 1881, married 6 May 1900, two children. Their son Ján, born 1906, and his wife Alžbeta from Porostov, married 1928. The grandmother Anna Kňežo, Roman Catholic, born at Iňačovce on 28 May 1850, married in September 1868 and a widow since September 1928; two children, one dead. A third household: Anna Ivanko, born 1902, and her son František, born 1928.

## Notes

- One of only two Greek Catholic Kňežo houses in the twelve sheets, and both have Roman Catholic wives. The 'grandmother' is of an age to be the owner's mother; her husband, a Kňežo, died in September 1928.
- She is Roman Catholic and was born at Iňačovce in 1850, thirteen years before the Roman Catholic widow Kočišová who is taken to be Mária Knyezső (DM-H-041). The same village, rite and generation; no tie is shown.
- The wife's birthplace is the same word as on the sheet for no. 129; possibly Laškovce. Asked of the Places agent.
- Columns 9 and 10 are 'all children born alive' and 'of them dead', for the last marriage. Read so here from this batch on.
- Anyone born after November 1926 is given by year only.
