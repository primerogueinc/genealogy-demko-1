## Translation

No. 7. 28 February 1867. Josephus; parents Joannes Szmeriga (?), farmer, and Susana Demko. Jesztreb 19. Male, 6. Smallpox. Buried at Jesztreb on 2 March by Josephus Danilovics, parish priest of Solymos.
