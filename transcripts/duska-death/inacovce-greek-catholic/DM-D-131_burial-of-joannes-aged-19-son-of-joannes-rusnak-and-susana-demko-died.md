## Translation

No. 22. 13 December 1864. Joannes; parents Joannes Rusnak (?) and Susana Demko. Jesztreb 18. Male, 19. Typhus. Buried at Jesztreb by Josephus Danilovics, parish priest of Solymos.

## Notes

- A printed form headed MATRICULA DEFUNCTORUM, page 106. The file is named for a Joannes Demko. On the strip the dead youth is not a Demko: Demko is his mother. A Susana Demko of Jesztreb is the wife of János Szmeriga in the baptisms of [1845] and of 13 November [1864] (DM-D-053, DM-D-036) and in the burial of 1867 (DM-D-137). Here, a month after that baptism, the father's surname reads Rusnak (?). Either there were two women of the name at Jesztreb, or Rusnak ('the Rusyn') was a by-name of the Szmeriga house. The strip does not say.
- Read from the image before the Duska agent's entry for this strip was opened; the first readings were fixed at 2026-11-12T22:28:12Z (tools/first-readings-DM-B-018.py). Then compared with DUS:D-013-50 @9462513.
- After comparison. Year: first read here as 1867 (?). Looked at again beside the strip of February 1867, the last figure is a 4, not that strip's 7. The file name and the Duska agent's reading (1864) stand.
- After comparison. Father's surname: first read here as Szmeriga, with the help of the baptism strips. Letter by letter it is nearer Rusnak, which is the Duska agent's reading. Adopted with a question mark.
- The Places agent's note on the word Rusyn (PL-S-137) says that in eastern Slovakia 'Rusnak' was a common word for a Greek Catholic. That makes a by-name more likely than it would be for another surname; it does not show it.
