## Translation

No. 27. 3 May. Mihály; his father György Demkó, farmer. Viszoka 121. Male, one month (?). Weakness. Buried at Viszoka on 5 May.

## Notes

- A second dead Mihály of György Demkó, farmer, of house 121: the house where János (1875) and Borbála ([1879]) were born to György Demkó and Mária Hreskó. The file name says 1888. The strip does not show a year, and its form is against 1888: it is the form of the strip that is proved to be 1879 (DM-D-117), with "atyja:", a place-and-house column and no year in the entry, while the page of 1883 (DM-D-120) writes the year in every entry in another hand. If it were 1888 the mother, who was bearing in January 1857, would have borne a child thirty-one years later, and the couple would have named a son Mihály while the Mihály of 1882 was alive. So the likelier reading is a year between 1876 and 1881, perhaps 1878 with one figure changed. It is not proved. If 1888 is right, there was a second, younger György Demkó, farmer, at house 121.
