## Translation

No. 1. 1 January 1857. Maria Demko, wife of György Tirpák. Vysoka 34. Female, 60. Consumption (?). Buried at Vysoka on 3 January by the parish priest of Csicser.

## Notes

- In Cyrillic, like the baptism of January 1857 in this book (DM-D-012): the book is in Cyrillic in the 1850s. The Places agent finds the same in the Solymos book for 1851 to 1853 and takes it as the eparchy's habit in those years, not as a sign of what the village spoke (its note 'What Rusyn means', PL-S-137). She is the Mária Demko, wife of György Tirpák, whose children were baptised from [1832] to [1839] (DM-D-024 to DM-D-026). Born about 1797: the oldest Demko of Viszoka with an age in this file. She is not the infant Mária Demkó of DM-D-012, who was born the next day.
- Read from the image before the Duska agent's entry for this strip was opened; the first readings were fixed at 2026-11-12T22:28:12Z (tools/first-readings-DM-B-018.py). Then compared with DUS:D-013-23 @9462513.
- After comparison. Husband's surname: this agent read 'M[...]nak' and could not finish it. The Duska agent reads Tirpák. In Cyrillic cursive the letter т is written like a Latin m; read so, the word is Тирпака. Adopted.
