No. 44. Born 24 February, baptised 27 February, year not on the strip (the owner's file name says 1892): Mária, daughter of Mihály Demkó, Roman Catholic, cottar, and Anna Batrovszki (?), Roman Catholic. The strip ends after the parents; no house, godparents or priest.
