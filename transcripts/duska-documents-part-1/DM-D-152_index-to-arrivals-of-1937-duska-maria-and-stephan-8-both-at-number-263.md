## Translation

A typed alphabetical index headed 1937. Duska, Maria, aged 31 (?), and Stephan, aged 8, both against the number 263.

## Notes

- Not an independent reading: the owner's file name says what the card is. Read from the image; the Duska agent reads the same, and also notes that the first figure of her age looks like a 5.
- Maria Demko was 31 in November 1937 and her son 8. Both share one number, as a mother and child travelling on one passport would. It is taken to be the page or sheet of the passenger list.
- The index names no ship, port or day. It shows that they were entered among arrivals in Canada in 1937, which the papers of DM-B-014 could not: those end with a ticket.
