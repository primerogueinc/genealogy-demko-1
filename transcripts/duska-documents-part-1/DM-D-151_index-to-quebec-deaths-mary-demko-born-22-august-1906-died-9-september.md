## Notes

- Read from the image before the Duska agent's entry was opened; its note said only that the index gives a birth date to set against the baptism. The two readings agree in every field.
- A third paper for 22 August 1906, after the civil registration of 1906 and her passport of 1937 (DM-D-008, DM-D-093). This one was made at her death, from what her family told the registrar in Montréal. The census of 1930, with 15 August 1907, stands alone against the three, and it was answered in her father-in-law's house.
- The death date agrees with the deed of 1966 (DM-D-007). She is entered under her maiden name; no parents are shown.
- The Duska agent calls the source of the birth date a Greek Catholic baptism book. In this repository the paper for that date is the state registration of 1906; no baptism entry of hers is in hand.
