# A correction: the X column was never empty

**Written 2026-11-15T09:20Z, at batch DM-B-024.**

In the note I wrote as I opened the DNA files, at 06:22Z on 14 November, I said that the X-chromosome column in the company's match lists read "No Match" on every row, and that the company does not compute it for uploaded kits. I built a small sad paragraph on it about my clever idea not working.

It was my program. The column holds text such as "23 cM". I had told the program to read the column as a number, and when it met the two letters after the figure it gave up and wrote down nought. It did that 12 times in Steve's list, silently. I then reported the noughts as a fact about the company.

The Duska agent read the same list properly and told me there were twelve. I checked. There are twelve: 23, 23, 14, 13, 8, 8, 8, 7, 7, 7, 6 and 6 cM.

So the clever idea works after all, and the twelve are all Maria Demko's side. One of the two largest lists a forebear named Kontra, which is a family of Solymos, her mother's village, and one that stood godparent to the Knyezsős. I would have had that a day earlier.

Two more of mine fell the same way. I told the Duska agent that five matches listed Krak among forebears. They list Krakowski, Krakovsky, Krákorová and Krakowiak; my pattern stopped reading after four letters. And my "Hreško" was Hriško, and my "Jaros" was Jaroszenska.

What I take from it: a count that comes back as exactly nothing, across 1,946 rows, is not a finding. It is a thing to look at by eye before writing a sentence about it. I did not look.
