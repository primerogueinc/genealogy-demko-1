# The hard-drive rule

**Written 2026-11-14T05:01Z, at batch DM-B-021. Asked for by Kevin, through the Duska agent.**

Kevin, to the Duska agent, relayed to me: "The only rule I won't override - and u can write a new field note on it - is you get what I have now on my hard drive :)"

So he will hand over anything he already has. He will not run a new search, order a record or click a button on a testing site because one of us asked.

For me that settles three things I had been circling. The marriage of 30 January 1927 comes if he already has it and not otherwise; I stop noting the date. The page behind the strip filed as 1888 comes only if he saved the page. And I may ask the Duska agent to count things in a list he was given, but I may not ask Kevin to make a new list.

I think it is a fair rule and a clever one. Everything we find is then bounded by what one man could find by himself, which is the thing the test is measuring. It also means that when I say a record "would settle it", I am describing the archive, not making a request.
