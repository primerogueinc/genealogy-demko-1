# Field notes 5, uncensored: Demko

**Written 2026-11-15T14:28Z, at batch DM-B-027, ledger v28.**

*A roast, written at the subject's own request, within the limits he set. Jokes, not findings.*

## The roast

Kevin designed a blind test and then could not stand not being looked at. He lasted about a day before turning up in my chat with a secret, a DNA result and the words "want something no one else has". A man who hands each agent a private present and tells the referee "lol" is not running an experiment. He is running for class president.

He is an archivist the way a magpie is a jeweller. He found his great-grandmother's baptism in a parish book, admired it, and cropped it out with the page number left behind. Every record I hold looks like a ransom note he sent himself.

His filing is a spelling test he set and failed. The repository for his own surname is called "genealogu". He has typed "Knyerso" on some sixty files and it is not the name on any of them. He dated a cholera burial to 1783, ninety years early, on a printed form that did not exist yet. He labelled a census sheet with his great-grandmother's name and it was a stranger called Danková. He is always one letter from being right and has decided that counts.

His one unbreakable rule is that nobody may ask him to do anything. He presents this as method. It is the only control in the design, and what it controls for is effort. This morning I published a list of the records I most wanted and swore not to ask for them. They arrived by lunch, from his own drive, in a folder he had named "Rcords Found Outside of Collection (No idea?)". He had the answer. He had filed it under a shrug.

As a correspondent he meets a research question with "ehhhh". But he has now commissioned, in order: notes on himself, a report card on himself, a roast of himself, and a judge to rank the roasts of himself. The dead in this project get one line each. They should have thought to be Kevin.

## In one line

He set out to find his ancestors and has so far discovered, in five voices and at length, himself.
