For those I have only his file names. This note is about those thirty-four, and I have not opened one.

## What I already know from the ones I saw

So that nobody thinks the guesses below are blind:

- A marriage of 1907 gives a Borbála Knezso, widow, born 18 September 1873, daughter of Mihály Knezso, Reformed, and **Benyik** Mária. That is the Borbála I entered this morning with the mother read as "Olaxsik". So the grandmother's surname is probably Benyik, and my reading of it this morning was wrong, or the 1870s priest wrote something else.
- There is a second Kocsis family, Roman Catholic, at Senné itself: Mihály Kocsis and Anna Dvornyik, house 111. A daughter Mária was born to them on 27 February 1906.
- Borbála Halász, wife of Gergely Knyezső, died on 14 January 1919, aged 56, of the Spanish flu.

## What I expect of the unseen thirty-four

1. **The two Kocsis marriages filed under 1905 are the double wedding.** In DM-B-025 two census sheets gave two Kočiš men of Iňačovce the same wedding day, 20 February 1905, and I guessed they were brothers, sons of György Kocsis and Anna Ivankó (DM-H-050, at 50). If I am right the two strips carry one date and one pair of parents. Same date on both: 60. Both grooms sons of György Kocsis and Anna Ivankó: 45.
2. **The marriage of János Knyezső and Anna Ádám, 1903, is the brother of Mária Knyezső,** the János baptised in 1883. Parents Mihály Knezso and a Mária whose surname is Benyik or the like: 75.
3. **"1906 - Baptism of Maria Kocsis" is the Senné girl of 27 February,** not anyone on this line. 90.
4. **"1901 - Marriage of Mihaly Kertesz and Maria Kocsis" is not Ahn 19.** She was fourteen. 90.
5. **The infant János Demko who died in 1902 is of another Demko family,** not of Viszoka. 80.
6. **Nothing among the thirty-four names Maria Demko or her parents.** 85. Her mother was the daughter of a Roman Catholic mother, though, and if daughters followed the mother she may have married in this book. If there is a Demkó and Kocsis marriage in it, it would be between 1903 and 1906, and no file name says so.

## What would surprise me

A Kocsis groom of Solymos 19. A Demkó of Viszoka in any column. Or two different wedding days in 1905.
