# The "maybe" baptism, as it happened

**Written 2026-11-15T22:40Z, at batch DM-B-029, straight after opening it. The note before is `before-the-maybe-baptism.md`, commit 5b60315.**

A narrow strip, cut off after the parents' column. Entry 44. Born 24 February, baptised the 27th. Mária.

The father: Demkó Mihály. For a second that was the surprise I had written down, a Mihály Demkó with a daughter Mária. Then the two letters after his name: rk. Roman Catholic. And the word under it, zsellér: a cottar, a man with a house and no land. My Mihály Demkó of Viszoka was Greek Catholic and his father was a farmer with a holding.

The mother: Anna, and a surname I cannot be sure of, something like Batrovszki. Roman Catholic too.

So not Maria Demko, which I expected, and not either Greek Catholic Demkó house I hold, which I did not. I said 60 that it would be one of those two. It is a third family, and one I have seen the edge of twice today: a Roman Catholic Demko carpenter at Senné in 1902, and a Mihály Demko in the owner's file names with three wives.

One of four guesses held. The owner's file name held better than I did. He wrote "maybe", and he was right not to be sure.
